# WW-Online 0.1.0

The first public release of WW-Online: online multiplayer for The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker. Everyone plays their own copy of the game in Dolphin, and WW-Online brings the other players into your world as fully animated Links and keeps your adventure in sync.

This is early development. Things work, and things break: expect bugs, and please report them.

## What it does

**See each other**
- Other players appear as real Links: running, rolling, sword combos, shield, ledge grabs and shimmying, ladders and vine walls, sidling, pushing and pulling blocks, crawling.
- They hold and use their items: bow (with aim), boomerang, hookshot, Deku Leaf, Skull Hammer, Wind Waker, bottles, telescope, Picto Box, Tingle Tuner, and a carried bomb with a burning fuse.
- Their sword, shield and clothes show, and each player picks a tunic colour that everyone sees change live.
- Each player's name floats above their Link (turn it off under **Appearance**).
- On the Great Sea you see each other sail, with the sail in their tunic colour, and their cannon or salvage crane when they take it out.

**Play one adventure together.** One player hosts a room and becomes the room owner, who picks what the room shares:

| Rule | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| **Shared wallet** | One rupee purse for everyone. |
| **Shared world** | Chests, switches and pickups are gone for everyone once someone takes them, live if you're in the same room: a chest opens empty, a bombed wall vanishes, a locked door comes back unlocked. Room puzzles (torches, crystal and floor switches, drop-down ladders) follow along for players in the same room. Small keys are shared too: a key anyone finds is everyone's, and a door anyone unlocks uses it up for everyone. |
| **Shared items** | One player finding an item unlocks it for the whole room, including heart containers and the magic meter. |
| **Shared story** | Main story progress is shared, so you can split up and finish different parts of the game. |
| **Other players' projectiles** | Other players' bombs, boat-cannon shots and arrows are real in your game: they fly, explode and hit your enemies and walls. They never hurt you. |

**Full sync** turns everything on. **Co-op** turns the four shared-progress rules off, so you see each other (and each other's projectiles) but keep your own progress. Joining a room never throws progress away: if you're further ahead, your progress is added to the room. Health, magic, bomb and arrow counts, and bottle and bag contents are never shared.

The Room page shows the room's rules, items, story events and a **Dungeons** card (small keys, map, compass, big key and boss for each dungeon).

## Requirements

- **Windows 10 or 11**, 64-bit.
- **[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org/download/)**, a recent development build (it comes with DolphinTool, which WW-Online uses to extract your disc image).
- **Your own copy of the game:** the US version of The Wind Waker, `GZLE01`, dumped from a disc you own (ISO, RVZ, GCM...). WW-Online contains no Nintendo code, files or assets. Please don't ask for, or share, game files.
- Everyone in a room needs this same version of WW-Online.

## Install

1. Download **`WWOnline-win-Setup.exe`** below and run it. It installs for your user only (no admin needed), and WW-Online updates itself when a new version comes out. Everything it needs, including .NET, is bundled.
   - The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"**. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
2. Open WW-Online. A short setup walks you through it: find Dolphin, choose your disc image (WW-Online extracts it and checks it's `GZLE01`), pick optional game patches (skip the intro, instant text, Swift Sail, crash fixes and more, from Better Wind Waker), your name and tunic colour, then **Patch**. The patched game goes in its own folder: your original is only read, never changed.
3. WW-Online needs Dolphin's 48 MB memory setting and turns it on by itself when it starts Dolphin for you. If you start Dolphin yourself: **Config → Advanced**, tick **Enable Emulated Memory Size Override** and set **MEM1** to **48 MB**.

WW-Online won't start or link up with a game that isn't patched and up to date. After an update that changes the game code, a banner asks you to patch again (**Patch now**).

## Playing together

- **Host a room** on the Room page: WW-Online starts the room on your PC (port `6969`) and starts your game. Friends enter your address and press **Join**.
- Over the internet, [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com/) is the easiest way (no router changes). Port forwarding TCP `6969` works too.
- Or run a **dedicated server** on any always-on machine or VPS, with Docker (amd64 and arm64):
  ```
  docker run -d --name ww-online --restart unless-stopped -p 6969:6969 ghcr.io/datstarkey/ww-online-server:0.1.0
  ```
  The [self-hosting guide](https://github.com/datstarkey/ww-online/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md) covers Docker Compose, the settings, the owner key (so the right player owns the room), firewalls and TLS. The `WW-Online-Server-0.1.0-*.zip` files below run the server without Docker.

## Known limitations

- You see up to three other players at once.
- Enemies aren't shared: each player fights their own. Rooms that open when every enemy is defeated need your own kills, except where the room's "cleared" switch opens something for everyone.
- Other players' boomerang and hookshot are shown in their hands, but the thrown boomerang, the hookshot chain and the grappling hook aren't. Carrying a pot or barrel shows the pose only.
- Other players' projectiles never hurt you, and players can't hurt each other.
- Room puzzle sharing (torches, crystal and floor switches, drop-down ladders) is new in this release, and the drop-down ladder case hasn't been tested in game yet. A few timed puzzles stay solved for the others once one player solves them.
- When another player hits a switch in your room, your game may play its own short cutscene for it.
- Two players opening the same chest at the same moment can both get its contents.
- Players bump into each other.
- A room's state lives in the server's memory: restarting the server empties it. Everyone's own game keeps its progress, and the room is filled again when players rejoin.
- Only the US version (`GZLE01`) is supported.
