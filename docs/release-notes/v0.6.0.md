# WW-Online 0.6.0

Side quests work together: counts, levels and prizes are the room's, Windfall's pedestals are shared, and a Heart Container another player took disappears from your screen. And there's now a [wiki](https://github.com/datstarkey/ww-online/wiki).

**Everyone needs 0.6.0**: it doesn't play with 0.5.x (new network protocol). **Patch the game again** after updating: the app asks you to (**Patch now**).

## New

- **Side-quest progress is shared** (Shared story). Progress the game keeps as a count or a level is now the room's, so nobody redoes it or gets paid for it twice:
  - **Mrs. Marie's Joy Pendants**: pendants anyone hands in count for everyone, towards the Cabana Deed and the Hero's Charm.
  - **Orca's lessons** and **Koboli's mail sorting**: everyone carries on from the room's level.
  - **Sploosh Kaboom** and **barrel shooting**: once anyone has won the Piece of Heart, the next win gives the Treasure Chart.
  - **The Ghost Ship**: once one of you clears it, it's gone for everyone, and so is its icon on the sea chart.
  - **Rose's pigs**: a pig anyone puts in the pen is in everyone's pen.
- **Windfall's pedestals are shared** (Shared delivery bag). A trade good one of you sets on a pedestal stands there for everyone, and anyone can take it back into the bag, so you can split decorating the town between you. If two of you use the same pedestal at the same moment, only the first counts and the other gets their item back.
- **The wiki**: a player guide covering installing, playing together, every room rule, the game patches, softlocks, self-hosting, troubleshooting and known limitations: <https://github.com/datstarkey/ww-online/wiki>.

## Fixed

- **Mrs. Marie took your Joy Pendants twice.** With the shared spoils bag, the second player to talk to her handed over 20 more pendants for a Cabana Deed the room already had.
- **Heart Containers stayed after another player took theirs.** When you both beat a boss, the container still waiting on your screen now disappears as soon as the other player picks theirs up. (Your hearts were already right: the room counts each container once.)
- **Winning a Piece of Heart twice.** Orca, Sploosh Kaboom and barrel shooting could show a second player a Piece of Heart the room already had, then take it away again.

## Known limitations

- An item set on or taken off a Windfall pedestal by another player shows on your screen the next time you come to Windfall.
- Two players handing Joy Pendants to Mrs. Marie at the very same moment: only the larger hand-in counts. Hand them in one at a time.
- Everything from 0.5.0's list still applies. The full list is on the wiki: [Known limitations](https://github.com/datstarkey/ww-online/wiki/Known-Limitations).

## Dedicated server

```
docker run -d --name ww-online --restart unless-stopped -p 6969:6969 ghcr.io/datstarkey/ww-online-server:0.6.0
```

See the [self-hosting guide](https://github.com/datstarkey/ww-online/wiki/Self-Hosting). The `WW-Online-Server-0.6.0-*.zip` files below run the server without Docker. Update the server too: 0.6.0 players can't join a 0.5.x server.
