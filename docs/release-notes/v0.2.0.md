# WW-Online 0.2.0

Other players now move like the real thing, rooms can share the bait and spoils bags, and a new **Warp to player** gets you out when shared story progress leaves you stuck.

**Everyone in a room needs 0.2.0** (the network protocol changed), and everyone needs to **patch the game again**: the app asks you to after it updates (**Patch now**).

This release was built quickly and hasn't had a full play-through yet. Expect bugs, and please report them.

## New

**Every animation.** Other players used to stand still whenever they did something WW-Online didn't know about. Now:
- 73 more moves play properly, including the Z-target side hop, strafing around a locked-on enemy, knock-backs and getting up, fall damage, sliding down slopes, surfacing from a swim, parries and sword bounces, ice slips, putting on the Iron Boots, rope swings, Boko weapon swings, boarding and leaving the boat, and poses like talking, opening chests, holding up an item, opening doors, saluting and fainting.
- Anything else they do is copied straight from their game: their body animation, arms, face and hands.

**Shared bait bag** (new room rule). All-Purpose Bait and Hyoi Pears are one room total: when anyone buys, picks up or uses them, everyone's bag follows.

**Shared spoils bag** (new room rule). Joy Pendants, Skull Necklaces, Boko Baba Seeds, Golden Feathers, Knight's Crests and Chu Jellies are one room total, up to 99 of each, including what you sell or trade.

**Warp to player** (new room rule: **Allow warping**). Each player on the Room page's Players list has a **Warp to** button:
- In the same area as them, you're moved straight to where they stand.
- Anywhere else, their area loads for you at the entrance they came in through.

It's for getting unstuck: shared story progress only moves forward, so a player who is behind can lose the way on. The first known case: if one player leaves Outset with Tetra, the others can no longer board the pirate ship. **Warp to** the player who left. The new [softlocks page](https://github.com/datstarkey/ww-online/blob/main/docs/softlocks.md) lists the known cases and how to report new ones.

All three new rules are on in **Full sync** and off in **Co-op**. The room owner can change them under **Edit room**, and a dedicated server sets them with `WWO_SHARED_BAIT`, `WWO_SHARED_SPOILS` and `WWO_ALLOW_WARPING` (or `--no-shared-bait`, `--no-shared-spoils`, `--no-warping`).

## Fixed

- Other players no longer stay red after taking damage.

## Known limitations

- Props that go with an animation aren't shown on other players: the rope, the item held up after a chest, a Boko weapon in hand. Animations that come from cutscene data aren't copied.
- Some moves are a best guess from what the other player's game tells us: a knock-back always looks like a hit from the front, and a side hop with the stick let go goes left.
- Bait, pears or spoils you only got from another player count as obtained, so you won't see that item's first-pickup message later.
- A warp to another area can't be done until the other player has come in through a door or loading zone (not after a void-out or respawn). If their entrance plays a cutscene, it plays for you too.
- Everything from 0.1.0's list still applies: enemies aren't shared, players bump into each other, and a room's state lives in the server's memory.
