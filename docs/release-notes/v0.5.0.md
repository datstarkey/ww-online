# WW-Online 0.5.0

The other players on your sea chart, parked boats and boat colours, and the postboxes and sunken treasure shared.

**Everyone needs 0.5.0**: it doesn't play with 0.4.x (new network protocol). **Patch the game again** after updating: the app asks you to (**Patch now**).

## New

- **Other players on the sea chart.** Open the sea chart in the pause menu and everyone else on the Great Sea shows up as a marker like Link's, in their tunic colour and pointing the way they face (or their boat's way while sailing). It works on the world map and on a zoomed-in square, even for players too far away to see.
- **Parked boats.** Get off your boat on the Great Sea and the others still see it where you left it, empty. It disappears while you're indoors, like your own.
- **Boat colour** (Appearance page). Pick the colour of your King of Red Lions as the others see it: **Match tunic** (the default, like the sail already does), **Classic** red, or one of the tunic colours. Your own boat stays red on your screen.
- **Postboxes are shared** (Shared story). A letter one of you has read is read for everyone, so you don't each unlock the same letters. Its reward reaches everyone through the other rules: the treasure charts through the sea map, the heart pieces through your hearts, the Complimentary ID and Fill-Up Coupon through the delivery bag, rupees through the wallet.
- **Sunken treasure is shared, live** (Shared world). When one of you salvages a treasure (from a chart, a light ring, the octoroks and so on), it's gone for the others straight away, the light and the spot included, like a chest.

## Known limitations

- The zoomed-in sea chart only shows the others while it's on the square you're in (that's when it shows Link).
- Everything from 0.4.0's list still applies.

## Dedicated server

```
docker run -d --name ww-online --restart unless-stopped -p 6969:6969 ghcr.io/datstarkey/ww-online-server:0.5.0
```

See the [self-hosting guide](https://github.com/datstarkey/ww-online/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md). The `WW-Online-Server-0.5.0-*.zip` files below run the server without Docker. Update the server too: 0.5.0 players can't join a 0.4.x server.
