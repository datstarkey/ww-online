# WW-Online 0.7.0

Other players show up on the sea chart at last, your own boat wears your colours, and parked boats stay where they were left. Also, another player climbing out of the water no longer drowns you.

**Everyone needs 0.7.0** (new network protocol: 0.6.x can't join a 0.7.0 room) **and a re-patch** (Settings → Patch game, or the app asks on start).

## Fixed

- **Drowning when another player climbed out of the water.** The moment another player left the water, your breath meter emptied, and if you were swimming in deep water you drowned. Your breath is now yours alone. Other players' Links also stop speeding up their swim stroke when you're low on air.
- **Nobody on the sea chart.** The other players never showed on the sea chart (Up on the D-pad). Now everyone on the Great Sea shows as a marker like Link's, in their tunic colour and pointing their way, however far away they are.

## New

- **Zoom into any square on the sea chart** and you see whoever is in it, not just on the square you're in.
- **Your own boat in your colours.** Your King of Red Lions now looks to you the way the others see it: the hull in your boat colour (Appearance page: Match tunic, Classic, or a tunic colour) and the sail in your tunic colour.
- **Parked boats stay where they were left.**
  - When another player gets off their boat, you see it where it is, even after they walk or swim away: on the sea when you're near the boat, and in Tower of the Gods in the room it's in.
  - On the sea chart, a player who parked shows where they are, not at their boat.
