# WW-Online 0.4.3

More of the dungeons update while you're in the room, and a fix for double magic.

**Patch the game again** after updating: the app asks you to (**Patch now**). 0.4.3 plays with 0.4.0-0.4.2 (same network protocol), but everyone should update.

## Changed

- **More dungeon objects update live** (Shared world). These used to change for other players only after they left the room and came back. Now they change straight away:
  - Forbidden Woods: the plants on the doors, the hanging flower house, and a leaf pile.
  - Earth Temple: the statues that dissolve in mirror-shield light, and the Earth God's Lyric statues.
  - Wind Temple: the Wind God's Aria statue, and Makar's soil mounds (the tree shows up grown).
  - Dragon Roost Cavern: the flame lift drops once its ropes are cut.
  - Ganon's Tower: Phantom Ganon's door.

  The flower house, the flame lift and the trees wait until you step away from them before they change.

## Fixed

- **Double magic by mistake** (Shared items). Picking up the Deku Leaf when the room had already shared the magic meter gave you double magic, and then everyone had it. Max magic now follows what you've actually earned: normal magic with the Deku Leaf, double magic from the Great Fairy. A room that already has double magic from this bug keeps it until the room owner sets Magic back to **Normal** on the Room items page (or you start a new room).

## Known limitations

- Everything from 0.4.0's list still applies.
