# Game patches

When WW-Online patches your game it can add a few optional extras: quality-of-life changes (skip the intro, instant text, Swift Sail, faster animations) and fixes for crashes and softlocks in the original game. They come from [Better Wind Waker](https://github.com/WideBoner/betterww) (WideBoner, brainfubar and contributors), which builds on the [Wind Waker Randomizer](https://github.com/LagoLunatic/wwrando) (LagoLunatic).

## Choosing patches

Pick them in the setup's **Game patches** step, or later in **Settings**, **Game patches** (then **Patch game**). WW-Online asks you to patch again whenever your choice differs from your patched game. The defaults are the bug fixes plus skipping the intro movie.

Each player chooses their own. Most patches are **local only**: they change your game and nobody else notices. A few change the world, and are marked **All players should match**: agree on those with your room, or players with different settings will see the game behave differently.

A few patches can't be combined (the three sailing wind patches all change the same thing); the app unticks the others when you pick one.
