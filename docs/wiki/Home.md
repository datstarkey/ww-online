# WW-Online

**Online multiplayer for The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker.** Everyone plays their own copy of the game in Dolphin, and WW-Online brings the other players into your world as fully animated Links and keeps your adventure in sync.

![The Room page: the room owner editing the room rules](../screenshots/room-owner-edit.png)

> **You need your own copy of the game.** WW-Online contains no Nintendo code, files or assets. It patches the game on your PC from a disc image you dumped yourself: the US version of The Wind Waker (game ID `GZLE01`). Please don't ask for, or share, game files.

## Start here

1. **[Installing](Installing.md)**: download WW-Online, get Dolphin and patch your game. The app walks you through it.
2. **[Playing together](Playing-Together.md)**: host a room or join one, and reach your friends over the internet.
3. **[Room rules](Room-Rules.md)**: what the room shares (wallet, world, items, story, bags, projectiles, warping) and how.

## Guides

- **[Game patches](Game-Patches.md)**: the optional extras (skip the intro, instant text, Swift Sail, crash fixes...) and which ones everyone in a room should match.
- **[Softlocks and warping](../softlocks.md)**: stuck after another player moved the story on? Warp to them.
- **[Self-hosting](../self-hosting.md)**: optional. A dedicated server (Docker, a VPS, a Raspberry Pi) keeps a room up without the host. You don't need one when your friends can reach your PC.
- **[Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md)**: black screens, connection problems, missing players, and how to report a bug.
- **[Known limitations](Known-Limitations.md)**: what doesn't work yet.

## Status

WW-Online is in early development. Things work, and things break. Everyone in a room needs the same WW-Online version: the app updates itself, and tells you when your game needs patching again.

What's new in each version: [Releases](https://github.com/datstarkey/ww-online/releases).
